The old grandfather clock in the hallway chimed midnight, its heavy brass pendulum swinging with a comforting, rhythmic persistence. Outside, a gentle autumn rain tapped against the windowpanes, blurring the distant amber glow of the streetlights into soft watercolor washes. Sitting by the fireplace, Elena turned the page of her worn leather notebook, the smell of aged paper mixing with the earthy scent of burning pine. For a moment, the world felt entirely still, suspended in a quiet pocket of time where the chaos of the day could no longer reach her.
